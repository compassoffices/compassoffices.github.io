Staff ID Card — Philippines

Your photo and signature,
ready in ten minutes.

Your Compass Offices ID card is printed from two files you send us: one portrait and one signature. This guide shows exactly what each one needs to look like, so your card comes back clean and correct the first time.

2 files to send COGL Management Limited Inc. Alphaland Makati Tower

1 — Your ID photo

A plain, front-facing portrait in business attire. We crop it into the orange circle at the top of the card.

JPG or PNG · square 1:1 · min 1000 × 1000 px

2 — Your signature

Your normal signature in black ink on white paper, scanned or photographed and cropped close.

PNG preferred · min 1200 px wide · white or transparent
01

Your ID photo

The card prints your photo inside a circle about 25 mm across, ringed in Compass orange. Anything outside that circle is cut away — so frame it generously and keep yourself centred.

Send a square image. We crop the circle — you do not need to crop it yourself.

File format
JPG or PNG. Under 5 MB. No PDF, no HEIC, no screenshots.
Size
Square (1:1), at least 1000 × 1000 px. Larger is better — we can always scale down.
Age of photo
Taken within the last 6 months and looking like you do today.
Background
Plain white or light grey. Nothing behind you — no doors, plants, desks or other people.
Attire
Business attire, as you would meet a client: collared shirt or blouse, blazer optional.
Expression
Face the camera squarely. Eyes open and visible. Neutral or a light, natural smile.
Lighting
Even and soft, from the front. No shadow cast on your face or on the wall behind you.
Distance
Ask a colleague to shoot from about 1.5 m at eye level. Arm's-length selfies distort the face.
Accessories
No caps, sunglasses, headphones or lanyards. Clear glasses are fine if there is no glare. Religious head coverings are welcome.
Editing
No filters, beauty smoothing, stickers or heavy retouching. Colour, not black and white.

Please do

  • Stand about a metre in front of a plain, well-lit wall.
  • Use the rear camera of a phone — it is sharper than the front one.
  • Hold the phone level with your eyes, portrait orientation.
  • Take a few frames and send the sharpest one.

Please avoid

  • Cropping a group photo, an event photo or a passport scan.
  • Standing too close to the wall — it creates a hard shadow.
  • Tilting your head or turning your shoulders away.
  • Sending a photo taken from a video call or CCTV still.
02

Your signature specimen

This is the signature printed on the front of your card, so it should be the same one you use on payroll and contract documents. Sign once, clearly, and send us a clean scan.

Sign inside the box without touching the edges. Send the whole box — we trim it.

Paper
Plain, unlined white paper. No ruled lines, letterhead or watermarks behind the signature.
Pen
Black fine-liner or gel pen, roughly 0.5–1.0 mm. Dark blue is acceptable; the card prints it in black.
Size
Sign at your normal size inside an 80 × 30 mm box. Do not cramp it or stretch it out.
Capture
Scan at 300 dpi, or photograph flat from directly above in even daylight.
File format
PNG preferred (JPG accepted), at least 1200 px wide, under 5 MB.
Background
Pure white or transparent. No grey cast, no page shadow, no visible paper edge.
Cropping
Crop close to the signature with a small even margin all round.
Consistency
Use the same signature that appears on your employment and payroll records.

Please do

  • Sign two or three times and pick the cleanest one.
  • Let the ink dry before scanning so it does not smudge.
  • Use the scanner in the centre if your phone photo looks grey.
  • Check the thin strokes are still visible before you send it.

Please avoid

  • Signing with a finger on a phone or tablet screen.
  • Pencil, light ballpoint, or a thick marker that bleeds.
  • Photographing at an angle, or with your own shadow across the page.
  • Reusing a signature lifted from a scanned contract or PDF.

You only send one signature. The Authorized signature on the reverse of the card is added by HR — nothing is needed from you for that field.

1

The circular crop is unforgiving

The corners of your square photo are discarded. If your head sits near an edge, the circle will clip your hair or an ear. Centre yourself and leave room.

2

The orange ring sits tight to your photo

A busy or dark background reads as a smudge against the ring. A plain white or light grey wall keeps the card looking crisp.

3

The signature prints small

It lands at roughly 20 mm wide. Faint or hairline strokes disappear at that size, which is why we ask for a dark pen and a 300 dpi scan.

4

Everything else comes from HR

Your name, designation, employee number and the details on the reverse are pulled from your employment record. Let HR know if anything has changed.

04

Sending your files

Attach both files to one email. Please do not paste them into a document or share them as a link.

WhenWithin 5 working days of your start date, or as advised by HR.

Before you hit send

  • Photo is square and at least 1000 × 1000 px
  • Background is plain white or light grey
  • Face is centred, level, and fills about 60% of the frame
  • No cap, sunglasses, filter or heavy retouching
  • Signature is in black ink on unlined white paper
  • Signature is cropped close, with no page edge or shadow
  • Both files are named correctly
  • Both files are attached to the same email

Not sure whether a photo will work? Send it anyway with a short note — we would much rather check it with you now than reprint the card later. Thank you for helping us get it right.
